01 — The Brief

Carnaby Street, 1968.
Fuzz, Feedback & Wild Velvet.

Fun, feel-good, gritty British Rock with hints of Psychedelic Surf vibes. Mid-tempo, rowdy, sexy, reckless. Built for advertising, ad films, and brand content that wants swagger instead of polish. Think a Tarantino car-chase scored by a band that lives above a London chip shop in 1969 — mod tailoring, dirty fuzz pedals, retro psychedelic groove with a wink.
